Hello! I'm Lu Jia (贾路), currently an undergraduate student in Computer Science and Technology at the University of Science and Technology Beijing (USTB). I have been admitted by recommendation to the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CASIA), for graduate studies.

Previously, I worked on evaluating large language models and agents, with experience in agent safety, theory of mind, and multi-agent reasoning. My current research interests are multimodal perception and embodied intelligence.
